Support

Get help with FlashBill

FlashBill is UAE VAT-compliant invoicing for small businesses — tax invoices, credit/debit notes, quotations, delivery notes, purchase orders, GRNs, bills, and payments, working fully offline with automatic sync.

Contact

Email flashbillae@gmail.com for account help, bug reports, or feature requests. Include your account email and, if it's a bug, what you were doing when it happened — that's usually enough to reproduce it quickly.

Want to delete your account? See Delete Your Account for how to do it in the app, or by email if you don't have the app installed.

Common questions

Why does a document I created offline show a real number right away?
FlashBill pre-reserves a small batch of real, sequential document numbers while your device is online, so a document created with no connection is still fully valid and final immediately — it doesn't wait for sync to become a "real" invoice. It syncs to the server automatically the next time you're online.

Can I run more than one business from the same login?
Yes — go to Settings → Switch / Add Business to add a second business under your existing email and password, or switch between businesses you already belong to.

How do I cancel an invoice I made by mistake?
Open the document and use Void — FlashBill never deletes or renumbers an issued document (UAE FTA requires the numbering series to stay sequential and gap-free), so voiding is the correct way to cancel one. The original stays visible, clearly marked VOID, and a new document can be issued in its place.

Is my data private from other businesses using FlashBill?
Yes — every business's data is fully isolated at the database level. See the Privacy Policy for details.
