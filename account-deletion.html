Delete Your Account

Two ways to permanently delete your FlashBill account and data

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

Open FlashBill and sign in, then go to Settings → Danger Zone → Delete My Account. Confirm once, and your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Without the app

Don't have the app installed, or can't sign in? Email flashbillae@gmail.com from the address on your account (or state the account email clearly) and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm once it's done, normally within a few business days.

What gets deleted

Your login, profile, and any business you personally own that has no issued documents yet are permanently erased. A business you own that does have issued invoices or other documents is archived rather than erased — UAE Federal Tax Authority rules require VAT records to be kept for 5 years (7 years for Corporate Tax records) regardless of whether the account that created them still exists, so that data is retained in archived form, not deleted, and is no longer accessible to anyone once your login is gone. If you're staff on someone else's business rather than the owner, deleting your account only removes your own access — that business and its data are unaffected.

Undoing this

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. There is no recovery period.
