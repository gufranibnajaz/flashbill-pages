Privacy Policy

Effective August 4, 2026 · FlashBill (UAE VAT invoicing app)

FlashBill is built for small businesses in the UAE to issue FTA-compliant invoices, credit notes, and related documents, and to keep the records those documents require. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and what you can do about it — in plain language, matching exactly what the app actually does, not a generic template.

Who this policy covers

FlashBill is developed and operated by Gufran Ajaz Shaikh, an individual developer, not a registered company. If you're a business owner using FlashBill, you are the "data controller" for the customer and transaction data you enter — FlashBill stores it on your behalf so you can run your business and meet UAE recordkeeping requirements.

What information FlashBill collects

  • Account details — the email address and password you sign up with, plus your name and phone number if you provide them.
  • Business details you enter — company name, TRN, address, and contact details entered in Company Setup.
  • Business records you create — customers, suppliers, items, invoices, credit/debit notes, quotations, delivery notes, purchase orders, GRNs, bills, and payments. This is the core data the app exists to manage.

FlashBill does not access your device's camera, location, contacts, or photos, and does not include any advertising or analytics/tracking SDK. Nothing is collected beyond what's listed above.

How your data is stored

All account and business data is stored in a managed Postgres database and file storage bucket provided by Supabase, FlashBill's only backend provider. Every business's data is isolated at the database level (Row Level Security) — one business's login can never read another business's records, including staff accounts within the same business only seeing that business's data.

Documents you choose to share (via the in-app "Scan QR" or "Copy Link" actions) generate a PDF that's uploaded to storage and made reachable by a unique, hard-to-guess link — it is not publicly listed or searchable, but anyone who has the link can open it, so treat a shared link like you would a printed copy of the document.

How your data is used

FlashBill uses the information above only to:

  • Run the app's core functionality — creating, numbering, storing, and displaying your documents.
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Let the app work offline and sync your changes back once you're reconnected.

FlashBill does not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers. Nothing is used for purposes beyond running the app you're using.

Data retention

UAE Federal Tax Authority rules require VAT records to be kept for 5 years, and Corporate Tax records for 7 years — FlashBill keeps issued/voided documents for that reason, and never lets a document number be silently deleted or renumbered (only voided, which keeps the full record intact). Account data is kept for as long as your account is active.

Your choices

  • You can edit or remove customer, supplier, and item records directly in the app at any time.
  • To request deletion of your account and its data (subject to the legal retention periods above for issued tax documents), contact support.
  • You can stop using the app and sign out at any time from Settings.

Third parties

Supabase (database, authentication, and file storage) is the only third-party service FlashBill's backend relies on. Standard app-store and device-level services (Apple/Google's own OS-level crash reporting, if any, and your device's native share sheet when you send a PDF) are outside FlashBill's control and governed by Apple's and Google's own privacy policies.

Children

FlashBill is a business tool and is not directed at, or knowingly used by, children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date above.

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact FlashBill Support.
